## Using these templates

Use this reference only after applying the authority order in `SKILL.md` and the core rules in `guide-rules.md`. These templates are a corrected, dissertation-focused digest of the user-supplied 12th-edition Section G and verified portions of `harvard-referencing-handbook-v2.md`.

Replace placeholders only with metadata present in or verified for the source. Do not force an entry into a template when its publication type is uncertain. Preserve the entry and flag the uncertainty.

Under the UEL profile, list every named author in the reference list. Use the first author plus italicised *et al.* only in the in-text citation for four or more authors.

## Journal articles and reviews

### Journal article with DOI

`Surname, Initials. and Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Article title', *Journal Title*, volume(issue), pp. first-last. Available at: https://doi.org/...`

If the journal uses an article number:

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Article title', *Journal Title*, volume(issue if present), article number 123456. Available at: https://doi.org/...`

### Journal article with URL but no DOI

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Article title', *Journal Title*, volume(issue), pp. first-last. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

### Print journal article

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Article title', *Journal Title*, volume(issue), pp. first-last.`

### Advanced online, pre-proof, or in-press article

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Article title', *Journal Title* (in press/pre-proof). Available at: DOI-or-URL [access date only when required by the controlling source-specific template].`

Do not invent final volume, issue, pages, or publication year. Update the entry only when the final publication metadata has been verified.

### Systematic review

Treat a systematic review as a journal article and retain its review-specific article identifier where supplied:

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Review title', *Journal Title*, volume(issue), article number or pages. Available at: https://doi.org/...`

Do not describe the dissertation's own structured review as a published systematic review unless that is factually accurate.

## Books and chapters

### Book

`Surname, Initials. (Year) *Book title: subtitle*. Edition when not first. Place: Publisher.`

For several authors, join the final name with `and` and list all named authors under the UEL profile.

### Ebook without print-equivalent pagination

`Surname, Initials. (Year) *Book title: subtitle*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Use a stable location, chapter, paragraph, or percentage in the in-text citation when pages are unavailable.

### Chapter in an edited book

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Chapter title', in Editor surname, Initials. (ed.) *Book title: subtitle*. Place: Publisher, pp. first-last.`

For several editors use `(eds)`. Preserve the editor-name order shown by the controlling guide.

## Conferences and theses

### Published conference paper

`Surname, Initials. (Year) 'Paper title', *Conference title*. Conference location, day-day Month. Place: Publisher, pp. first-last. Available at: DOI-or-URL (Accessed: date when required).`

Where the paper is published as a normal journal article, use the journal-article format rather than a conference template.

### Full published proceedings

`Editor surname, Initials. (eds) (Year) *Proceedings title*. Conference location, day-day Month. Place: Publisher. Available at: DOI-or-URL (Accessed: date when required).`

### Electronic thesis or dissertation

`Surname, Initials. (Year) *Title*. Degree thesis. Awarding institution. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

### Unpublished thesis or dissertation

`Surname, Initials. (Year) *Title*. Unpublished degree thesis. Awarding institution.`

Use the degree description printed in the source. Do not infer the degree or institution.

## Reports and official publications

### Government or official Saudi publication

`Responsible ministry or authority (Year) *Title: subtitle*. Report or document number when supplied. Place: Publisher.`

For online material:

`Responsible ministry or authority (Year) *Title: subtitle*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Use the responsible organisation as corporate author. Add a country qualifier only when needed to distinguish organisations from different countries. Use an official English organisation/title where supplied; otherwise apply the non-Roman-script rule in `guide-rules.md`.

### International organisation publication

`Organisation (Year) *Title: subtitle*. Publication identifier when supplied. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

### Research report or working paper

`Surname, Initials. (Year) *Title: subtitle*. Series and report/working-paper number. Place or institution: Publisher. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Omit elements that the controlling template does not require; never manufacture a place, series, or number.

### Company report

`Company (Year) *Title: subtitle*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Use the legal or published corporate name consistently. Distinguish a company report from a normal web page and from database-generated company data.

### Market or financial database report

`Database provider (Year) *Report title*. Available at: database or stable URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Record the database and report actually consulted. Do not present database content as the company's own report.

## Standards and datasets

### Technical standard

`Standards body (Year) *Standard number: Standard title*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Retain the exact standard number, edition/year, amendments, and issuing body. Flag supersession or currency separately; do not silently replace the cited version.

### Research dataset

The supplied 12th-edition extract gives an abbreviated dataset example rather than a complete general schema. When no higher guide controls, use only the verified available elements:

`Creator(s) (Year) 'Dataset title'. Available at: DOI-or-URL (Accessed: day Month year when the link is not a permanent DOI).`

If repository, version, edition, publisher, or `[Dataset]` designation is required by the institution but absent from the source, report it as missing rather than inventing it.

## Web pages

### Individual author

`Surname, Initials. (Year) *Web-page title*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

### Corporate author

`Organisation (Year) *Web-page title*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

### No identifiable author

`*Web-page title* (Year) Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

### No date

`Author or organisation (no date) *Web-page title*. Available at: URL (Accessed: day Month year).`

Never substitute the homepage title for a specific page title when the latter is available. Do not cite the bare URL in text when an author or title can be identified.

## Classification safeguards

- A downloaded PDF retains the source type of the publication it contains.
- A repository copy of a journal article normally remains a journal article; distinguish a preprint or accepted manuscript when final publication details are unavailable.
- A government web page is not automatically a government report.
- A report hosted on a company website remains a report, not a generic web page.
- A conference paper published in a journal uses the journal format when the journal is the cited publication.
- An institutional repository record is not necessarily the work itself. Cite the work actually read.
- When a DOI and ordinary URL are both present, prefer the DOI unless the controlling guide says otherwise.
- Do not use templates for legal, artistic, social-media, personal-communication, or other excluded specialist material. Flag those cases for confirmation against the controlling guide.
